Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 1 September 2026

1. Appointments

Appointments booked through stoepbarbers.co.za are held for the customer for the selected date, time, service and barber. We recommend arriving 5 minutes before your scheduled start time to allow for a prompt start.

2. Cancellations

We ask for at least 4 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. This gives us a fair chance to offer the slot to someone else. Cancellations can be made by phone or email using the details on our Contact page.

3. Late arrival

If you arrive more than 15 minutes after your booked start time, we may need to shorten your service to fit the remaining appointment window, or ask you to rebook for a later slot the same day if one is available. This is to avoid delaying customers booked after you.

4. No-shows

A no-show is a booking where the customer does not arrive and does not contact us. Repeated no-shows may result in us asking for a booking to be confirmed by phone before future appointments are held.

5. Payment

Payment is due at the time of service. We accept card, SnapScan and cash. No deposit is required to make a booking online.

6. Service changes

If you'd like to change the service you've booked (for example, adding a beard trim to a haircut) let your barber know when you arrive. Where time allows, we'll accommodate this; where it doesn't, we may suggest rebooking for the full combination.

7. Customer responsibilities

Please let your barber know about any skin sensitivities, allergies, or conditions that could affect your service before it begins. We rely on this information to work safely.

8. Personal information

When you book online we collect your name, email address, phone number and any notes you provide, in order to hold and manage your appointment. This information is used only for that purpose and to contact you about your booking — it is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing. You can ask us to delete your details from our records at any time by emailing us.

9. Website usage

This website and its content are provided for the purpose of informing customers about our services and enabling online bookings. Content may not be copied or reused for commercial purposes without our permission.

10. Liability

While we take care with every service, results can vary with hair type, growth pattern and previous colour or chemical treatments. We'll always discuss a style with you before starting. To the extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the value of the service booked.

11. Policy updates

We may update these terms from time to time to reflect changes to how we operate. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
