The short version

A neighbourhood barbershop that takes the work seriously.

Stoep opened on Duncan Street in 2016 with two chairs and a coffee machine that took a while to figure out. Nine years on, the coffee's better and the standard hasn't moved: a cut should still look right in three weeks, not just when you walk out the door.
