Services & pricing

Every price includes a hot towel finish. Durations are per chair time — book the slot that matches what you're having done.

Good to know

Before you book

Running late?

Call ahead. More than 15 minutes and we may need to shorten the service or move your slot — see our terms for details.

Picking a barber

Each barber follows the same price list. If you don't have a preference, choose "no preference" at booking and we'll assign the next available chair.

Card & cash

We take card, SnapScan and cash. Payment is on the day — no deposit needed to hold your slot.
