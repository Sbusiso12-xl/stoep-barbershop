The story so far, and the people cutting the hair.

How it started

Neo Mahlangu spent five years cutting hair in a barbershop in Sunnyside before deciding he wanted to do it his own way. In 2016 he found a small unit on Duncan Street with a covered stoep out front — the kind of spot where you end up chatting to whoever's waiting next to you. The name stuck before the shop even had a sign.

The idea was simple: do the work properly, don't overbook the day, and make sure a cut still looks sharp two or three weeks later, not just when the client stands up from the chair. Nine years on, that's still the whole philosophy.

What we're not

We're not trying to be the fastest barbershop in Hatfield, and we're not chasing every trend that shows up online. We do a smaller set of services well, we keep good notes on regulars, and we'd rather tell you a style won't suit you than talk you into it.
